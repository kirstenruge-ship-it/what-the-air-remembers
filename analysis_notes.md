# Scientific interpretation commitments

Recorded while no prospective Kimi outputs existed. The primary outcome is the post-reasoning first native response opportunity in the frozen registration.

## Read-only development witness

Development arms record all six previously fitted attention-input sites on the same cue token (23, 27, 47, 51, 71, 75). The selected write site, its L+4 witness and the thresholds in the registration govern delivery. Upstream sites provide an expected-zero check on a cold matched prefill. For each available site we will show signed projection onto that layer's separately fitted odor displacement, total displacement, normalized scale and repeat-sham magnitude. This is a descriptive trajectory of one-position writes. An interesting result at another layer cannot convert a failed registered gate into a pass.

## What the model said during reasoning

Raw data include the complete emitted reasoning and final reply for every natural run. After the scientific lock, a descriptive sample can show whether Kimi spoke about a missing target, structural chemistry, a printed source name, a particular episode, or a general preference. A written explanation is an additional output; it can rationalize a sampled decision. Reports of smelling, knowing, uncertainty or comfort do not constitute measurements of phenomenal experience. Do not score an auxiliary agent's emotion label as an experimental endpoint. The lexical and chemistry baselines, matched preference crossover, physical write receipts, deletion and rescue carry the evidential weight.

## Three distinct claims

1. An existing pattern is tied to ordinary similarity judgments across aliases and survives nuisance checks. This is representational correspondence.
2. A novel unnamed write causally changes downstream native choices and a matched control fails to explain it. This is conditional causal use on the tested prompts and checkpoint.
3. Removing the same component selectively weakens an ordinary named judgment, and correct restoration helps more than a wrong restoration. This is evidence for participation in ordinary use.

Identifying whether a component originated in pretraining requires an earlier or matched base checkpoint. Demonstrating transfer from a physical sensor requires actual calibrated sensor recordings. An affect-tagged in-context retrieval test measures retrieval of an incident. A no-context reset with a durable store, and a human-safety assessment of erroneous retrieval, are separate milestones.

## Reliability of the native comparison target

For every existing pair of ordinary order-flipped natural comparisons, display both correctly oriented logits, their mean, their half-difference and sign agreement. Opposite signs represent a genuine order/history uncertainty. Keep both values and all prespecified targets in the primary denominator. A zero or unstable native preference is weak evidence for a molecule's identity, even if a chosen writer changes the odds. This is an existing-data sensitivity and makes no new requests.

## Absolute probability of an observed action token

Alongside relative A/B log odds, for each matched condition report the unconditioned probability of the bare letter that the target's named reference favors. It is the native A+B probability mass times sigmoid of the signed, order-corrected raw margin. A favorable relative margin at very small A+B mass could be behaviorally negligible. Keep sampled actions and malformed responses separate. This secondary estimate uses the same frozen inputs and recorded native distribution, without additional exposure or retuning. Native ties have no preferred letter and are explicitly accounted for.

## Fixed affect-tagged choice versus retrieval

For each of the 24 household stories, also orient the A/B margin toward the action tied to the third party who was relieved, then average over both cue identities and both history bindings. Compare the complete four-cell means under the odor, lexical and sham conditions. This separates a general tilt toward the relieved example from the registered cue-by-binding reversal. Report both. A change in an affect-tagged choice is not evidence that the model felt that emotion, nor does a design without a neutral-ending control test whether emotional significance improves memory.
