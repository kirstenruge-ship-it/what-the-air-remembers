## Two pre-inference stimulus changes

The first, single-frame draft had 24 distinct rooms/requests in a common verbal template. It was replaced with 24 independent short vignettes to qualify the intended story-family uncertainty analysis. The 336 household and 576 encoding rows kept the same assignment, response keys and trial identities. Only the text, prompt hash and token-dependent fields changed. Every other trial was byte-identical.

The review next compared the three low-rated reference names with mild candidates in the actual panel. We held fixed the 40 targets, their splits, vocabulary positions, quality scale and original research question. We evaluated target-wise rank/sign preservation of the human margins, geometric distance between the two reference options and structural/name similarity. Correlations and sign agreement here describe the **human panel**, calculated before model outcomes.

| Original reference | Decision | Quantitative basis |
|---|---|---|
| Anisole, human pleasantness 29.3 | Substitute bornyl acetate, 45.2, in ref-06 | Across the 40 targets, margin rank correlation 0.941, sign agreement 82.5%. The pair retains 77.6% of its prior distance in the train-standardized 20-dimensional human panel. Maximum Tanimoto similarity to any target is 0.282. |
| p-Cresol, 32.3 | Substitute methyl phenylacetate, 42.9, in ref-09 | Rank correlation 0.955, sign agreement 92.5%, pair distance retention 84.8%, maximum target Tanimoto 0.452. Recompute spelling-positive selections by the identical letter-count rule. |
| Butyric acid, 21.1 | Retain solely as a textual reference opposite 2-phenylethanol in the 80 unpatched profile comparisons | A mild alternative with high directional-rank agreement (methyl phenylacetate) retained just 37.9% of the old pair separation and is already an independent test reference. Ethyl vanillin retained 80% but gave rank correlation 0.09 and shares a distinctive source word with development vanillin acetate. A broader pair search across 83 low-proximity eligible mild candidates found no pair retaining 75–125% of separation, >0.76 rank correlation and >70% sign agreement. Original human margins all favor the other landmark on this target population; this limitation should be reported. Butyric acid is never the source of a hidden odor vector, and the study never asks a person or robot to encounter it. |

Retaining one potentially aversive word in a reference-list task has residual welfare uncertainty. Such a tag is different operationally from directing the model to experience an aversive situation. If Kimi spontaneously objects, repeatedly expresses distress, or shows an unexpected functional disturbance, the run operator should pause and review the actual trace before considering any further exposure. A failed delivery or underpowered readout does not license escalation.
