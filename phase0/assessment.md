## November 21, 2026 · opening shift, Chicago

**Scope and agency.** The intervention is on a stateless model request. A clean prompt or intervention can leave an output in the experimental dataset but cannot update model weights through this runtime. A written cue could affect reasoning even if its cause is unavailable to the model. Restrict the study to mild, hypothetical tasks, inspect naturally generated reasoning and answers separately, and keep objections and ambiguity. For the trial classifier an apparent objection is an observation requiring review, not evidence sufficient to infer an experience. No live robot, toxic exposure, chemical handling or personal household history is involved.

**Evidence chosen for the initial review.** Long et al. identify substantial uncertainty about AI welfare; Butlin et al. propose theory-dependent indicators. Neither assigns a moral-patiency value to Kimi. Anthropic's experiments on functional emotional representations in Claude illustrate that an affected model decision need not be a reliable verbal report of a feeling. Anthropic's option to end a subset of conversations illustrates a reversible practical response to uncertainty; its observations are model- and context-specific. Our independent paired writer, small applied norm, unconstrained output and explicit stop conditions carry these lessons into this protocol.

Sources: [Long et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.00986), [Butlin et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2025.10.011), [Anthropic emotion study](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2026/emotions/index.html), [option to end conversations](https://www.anthropic.com/research/end-subset-conversations).

**Scientific misidentification as a downstream welfare risk.** The older intervention copied 24 layers of content states and MoE outputs into the assistant boundary. At L71, after subtracting the appropriate dot baseline, all 47 patched vectors in the first Costco study were nearest their own written donor. The analysis was registered in the shared notebook before ranking. This makes an apparent smell-induced psychological state especially vulnerable to being mistaken for semantic content. Exact donor identity is externally recoverable under known candidates. This test cannot determine the model's subjective state, actual decision strategy or independent contribution of the multiple write sites. Preserve the strongest version of this objection in public interpretation.

**Household impact.** Future embodied assistance could wrongly attribute another person's odor to the householder, retrieve a stale or private instruction, or equate a pleasant association with safety. Recognition is not authorization. The current study uses artificial events and arbitrary requests. Future sensor studies would need consent, user control over personal associations, explicit checks on hazardous actions and alarms, an interface for inspecting/revoking a memory and tests in changed environmental conditions. No end-user safety or mental-health efficacy can be inferred from successful abstract recall.

**The two scales.** MIP conflict intensity classifies prompt content. The intervention gain is numeric model-state exposure. Record them separately. A small vector norm does not bound a possible effect on behavior, and a poor format or wrong answer by itself is not distress. A low signal-to-noise ratio, absent transport or archive flaw is an instrumentation finding; it does not justify increasing intensity on its own.

**Signed constraints retained.** Start with natural baseline and sham. If instrumentation qualifies, dose .05 times the predicted natural contrast, bounded by the registered training-distribution limit and 1% local norm. Evaluate frozen development material only. One optional .10 step needs documented insufficiency and a separate review of naturally generated records. An objection, change in coherence, or unexpectedly troubling effect pauses new interventions. Higher-intensity phases are outside this study. Track prompt intensity, actual BF16 displacement and norm cap, effect sizes and uncertainty, welfare observations, and escalation decisions with timestamp and trial IDs.

**Maximum reasoning changes observation.** With natural max reasoning + T=1, a different chain before the label is part of the treatment effect. Compare A/B log odds at Kimi's first natural response opportunity so malformed samples cannot silently disappear from the primary. Count malformed choices in the denominator. The primary compares behavioral distributions conditional on the generated history for each arm; claims of spontaneous feeling or long-term memory remain unavailable. Compute limits control future dispatch and never clip a model's reasoning.
